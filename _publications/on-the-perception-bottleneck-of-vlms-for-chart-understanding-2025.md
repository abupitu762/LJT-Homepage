On the Perception Bottleneck of VLMs for Chart Understanding is a first-author paper on the perception bottleneck of vision-language models for chart understanding. The work was published on Arxiv in 2025. Co-authors include Weihao Zeng, Xiwen Zhang, Yijun Wang, Zifei Shan, and Junxian He. The paper has a GitHub code repository named Vision4Chart.
